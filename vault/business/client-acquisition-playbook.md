## Your 3 strongest ICPs (who to sell to)

1. **Course & exam-prep creators** (edu-creators, cohort coaches)
   - Pain: their course is a passive PDF/video dump; completion is low and they blend in.
   - Why you close them: **BMLA Quest IS the deliverable** — XP, ranks, mock exams, leaderboard.
     Send the link, they feel it in 10 seconds.
   - Angle: *"Your course is a PDF people don't finish — I'll turn it into a game they can't put down. Here's one I built: bmla-quest.vercel.app"*

2. **Indie founders / agencies / personal brands** needing a cinematic landing page fast
   - Pain: flat Wix/template site; can't afford a $10k agency or a 6-week timeline.
   - Why you close them: **Empire** proves cinematic, animated, premium interactive web — the
     "$30k site" feel — shipped in days, fixed price.
   - Angle: *"Most landing pages whisper. I'll make yours feel like a movie trailer — live in days, not weeks. Proof: empire-rise.vercel.app"*

3. **Indie / Web3 game studios & brands** wanting playable web teasers / quiz funnels (warm upsell)
   - Pain: flat marketing site; ads people scroll past; want a playable funnel but can't source
     narrative + game loop + clean web in one person.
   - Why you close them: Empire (branching narrative) + BMLA Quest (game loop) together prove a
     playable branded experience end-to-end. Sell this as an upsell, not cold.
   - Angle: *"Stop running ads people scroll past — let them play your brand. See the engine: empire-rise.vercel.app"*

**Follow-up cadence (stop at 4 touches)**
- Day 0: initial message (lead with the playable link).
- Day 3 (same channel): "floating this back up — happy to just send the free 60-sec Loom so you can judge for yourself, no call needed. Want it?"
- Day 7 (add value, not "just checking in"): "Made this anyway — quick idea for [Brand]: [one concrete sentence]. If it's interesting I'll send the full Loom."
- Day 14 (switch channel once, break-up): "I'll stop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox — if a fast site/game is ever useful, my work's at empire-rise.vercel.app. Door's open anytime."

**Do / Don't**
- DO: clickable playable link in line 1–2 · personalize the opener to something real · offer something free+small (a Loom, one idea) instead of asking for a call · one CTA · send Tue–Thu in their timezone.
- DON'T: "I hope this finds you well" / a life story · a wall of text or multiple links/asks · "student"/"just starting out" framing · ask for a meeting as the first CTA · attach files to cold email · send "just checking in" twice.

- **Day 1 (Mon)** — Confirm Empire + BMLA load clean on mobile. List 10 Karachi businesses you know with weak/missing sites. Draft a "live in 5 days" flat package (PKR 30–80k). Set up Loom.
- **Day 2 (Tue)** — Record three 60-sec Looms for your 3 hottest local leads; send all 10 DMs (lead with Empire). Tue = best reply day; your single best shot at a dollar this week.
- **Day 3 (Wed)** — Publish 3 narrow Fiverr gigs (Empire+BMLA in gallery). Join Claude Devs + 1 indie-hacker + Devs-For-Hire Discord; help 2 people, no pitch.
- **Day 4 (Thu)** — Reply to 3–5 posts in the PH "Hiring?" + HN "Who is hiring?" threads with a flat price + Empire link. First build-in-public X thread with a GIF. Follow up quiet local leads.
- **Day 5 (Fri)** — 3–5 tailored Upwork bids (Framer/cinematic SaaS landing, Webflow). Apply to Teachable Experts. Email 3 Gumroad/course creators (BMLA Quest as proof).
- **Day 6 (Sat)** — DM 5 agencies offering a cheap white-label pilot. Build-log in Discord; reply to game-dev "looking for designer" posts ($300–800). Follow-ups.
- **Day 7 (Sun)** — Reply to every warm thread; send Day-7 add-value follow-ups; book any interested lead for the free Loom. Double down Monday on whatever got replies. Payoneer + Wise ready.

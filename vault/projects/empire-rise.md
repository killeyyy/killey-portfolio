## What it is
A narrative, choice-driven crime game in the browser. The player starts with nothing
and climbs to the top through decisions — branching paths, consequences, a cinematic
feel. Built to be played instantly in a browser, no install.

## The story (case-study seed: problem → build → shipped)
- **Problem / intent:** make an interactive story that *feels* like a film, runs anywhere,
  and shows off cinematic choice design — playable straight from the portfolio.
- **How it's built:** AI-first workflow — directed AI tools through writing, branching
  logic, UI and deploy. Shipped to Vercel as a static/SPA build.
- **What shipped:** a live, playable build embedded on the portfolio (`/work/empire-rise`).

- Featured as a flagship on the public site; playable embed (click-to-launch, sandboxed).

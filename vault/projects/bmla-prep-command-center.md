> One-liner (site): *A focused study dashboard — everything for exam prep in one calm command center.*

## What it is
A single calm dashboard for exam prep: the materials, tasks and progress for Business Maths
& Linear Algebra in one place, so studying has a home instead of being scattered. Sibling to
**BMLA Quest** (the gamified side); this is the focused, no-noise side.

## The story (case-study seed: problem → build → shipped)
- **Problem / intent:** prep was scattered across notes/files; centralize it into one
  dashboard that reduces friction and decision-fatigue.
- **How it's built:** AI-first; directed AI tools through layout, the dashboard data model,
  and deploy. Browser-first.
- **What shipped:** live at the URL above (note: the domain is `hassan-deals-pk.vercel.app`).

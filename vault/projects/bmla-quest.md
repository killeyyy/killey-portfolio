## What it is
A study tool that turns Business Maths & Linear Algebra revision into a quest — progression,
challenges, and a game loop layered onto real coursework so studying feels like playing.

## The story (case-study seed: problem → build → shipped)
- **Problem / intent:** make exam prep for BMLA actually engaging by reframing it as a game
  with progression and momentum, not a wall of practice problems.
- **How it's built:** AI-first — directed AI tools through content structuring, the quest/
  progression loop, UI and deploy. Browser-first, instant to open.
- **What shipped:** a live, playable build embedded at `/work/bmla-quest`.

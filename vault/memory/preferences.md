# Preferences (design, work, taste)

> Long-term preferences. Keep current; the site, Claude and Codex defer to this.

## Design / aesthetic
- **Cinematic, premium, flashy, colorful, impressive.** "$50K", award-worthy feel.
- Colorful **accents on a cinematic dark base** — ink black base, champagne/silver text,
  garnet→crimson lead, violet→cyan cool axis, gold detail, jade only for "live/shipped".
  Vivid but disciplined (max ~2 primary accents at once). Not a rainbow clown site.
- Animation and interactivity are wanted (motion, glow, gradients, a WebGL hero) — but it
  must stay accessible (reduced-motion) and not feel cheap. Choreography > effect-count.

## Work style
- Move fast and ship something playable/visible over endless concepting.
- I like the AI-first, multi-agent way of working (research → build → review → brief).
- Give me ready-to-paste next-step prompts and a clear "what to click" list.

## Taste (for product/content ideas)
- Power-fantasy / ascension stories, game-feel, competitive skill, cinematic flash.
  (See the games taste: CS/Valorant precision, MOBA last-hit/builds, MK combos,
  Solo Leveling / Demon Slayer / Black Clover power fantasy.)

## Hard "don'ts"
- No "Sabit Qadam" mantra. No student / IBA framing in public. No public location.
- No invented metrics or fake social proof. Never break the live site.
